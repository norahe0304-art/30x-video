# Video-Specific Anti-Slop Blacklist

These are video-format-specific rules that go BEYOND `design-rules/taste.md`.
Both files apply. If conflict: `taste.md` wins (it's the broader principle).

## Transition Slop (instant fail)

- Stock light leaks crossing the screen
- Lens flare bursts at scene starts
- Fade-to-black between every scene (use sparingly, max 2× per video)
- Cube / page-flip / 3D rotation transitions
- Glitch effects without narrative justification
- Whip-pan transitions in non-action content
- Particle bursts at every cut
- "Smooth zoom" (Sam Kolder bro-trip cliché) used in B2B context
- Same transition repeated 3+ times — variety > polish

## Pacing Slop

- 35-40s video stuffing 6+ scenes (luxury pacing requires hold)
- 15s video with only 2 scenes (social needs movement)
- Quick-hook video where text < 1.2s on screen (unreadable)
- Scenes faster than reading time × 1.5 buffer
- BGM drop without visual payoff
- Slow-mo > 2× duration without earned dramatic weight
- Hold duration unconnected to scene complexity

## BGM Slop (instant fail)

- Generic royalty-free corporate elevator pitch (the Bensound / Pixabay defaults)
- Music with vocal hooks fighting VO frequency band
- BGM not ducked under VO (-12 to -18 dB target)
- Tempo / BPM mismatched with pacing (slow-luxury + 140bpm techno is wrong)
- BGM cuts off mid-bar at video end (always fade out properly)
- Same BGM track used across multiple deliverables in same project
- BGM louder than VO peaks
- Sting / orchestral hit at random moments without purpose

## VO Slop (instant fail)

Forbidden phrases (hide weak writing):
- "Discover the future of..."
- "Welcome to a new era of..."
- "What if I told you..."
- "Imagine a world where..."
- "It's not just X, it's Y"
- "Revolutionary / game-changing / cutting-edge"
- "Elevate / unleash / empower / transform / reimagine"
- "All-in-one solution"
- "Best-in-class / industry-leading"

Delivery problems:
- Speed > 175 wpm (anxious)
- Speed < 130 wpm (sluggish, only OK for cinematic-luxury)
- No pause beats between sentences
- Voice generated by clearly default TTS (mechanical, no breath, no pacing variation)
- Final word lands ON the visual cut (always 0.3-0.5s before)
- VO competing with BGM peak (must duck)
- Too many filler words ("um", "you know", "kind of") if using user-recorded
- Voice gender / age mismatched with brand (e.g. corporate fintech with Gen-Z TikTok voice)

## On-Screen Text Slop

- Text > 12 words per scene (viewer can't read in time)
- Text appears in last 15% of scene duration (no time to read)
- Multiple competing text blocks fighting for attention
- Subtitle baked-in at the same time as headline (one OR the other, not both)
- Drop shadows + neon outlines + glow ALL on same text
- Text smaller than 28px at 1080p (unreadable on mobile)
- Auto-typewriter animation that doesn't finish before scene cuts
- All-caps headline in non-statement scenes (caps is for emphasis only)

## Visual Slop

- AI-generated faces that look almost-real-but-not (uncanny valley)
- Stock business photography (handshake / suit / smile combo)
- Generic AI-generated "diverse team" images
- Floating product against gradient (no environment context)
- Overlay opacity 50% reducing logo to mush
- Logo bug stuck in corner for entire video (use sparingly, only at start/end)
- Lower thirds with gradient pill backgrounds
- Counter animations that don't actually count (just flash final number)
- Charts that look impressive but are empty of data
- Decorative motion graphics signifying nothing (the "AI b-roll" problem)

## Format Slop

- 16:9 horizontal forced into vertical platform (TikTok, Reels, Shorts)
- 9:16 vertical with critical content in top/bottom 10% (cropped on some platforms)
- 1:1 square cropped from 16:9 losing key composition
- Safe zones violated for platform UI (TikTok's right-side rail eats 15% of screen)
- Master 16:9 + reformatted vertical with letterboxing instead of recomposition
- Captions baked-in to video without burnable subtitle file (bad accessibility)

## Closing Slop

- "Subscribe for more" CTA in non-creator content
- "Visit our website" with URL too small to read
- Logo mark + tagline + CTA + social handles ALL on final frame (one focal point only)
- Final frame held > 4s (anything past 4s feels stuck)
- Music ending mid-phrase (always fade or land on a cadence)
- "Thank you for watching" (cringe in any context)

## The Watch Test

After render, watch the video at 1× speed. Note every moment where:
- Your eye gets bored
- You think "I've seen this before"
- You can't tell what scene is supposed to communicate
- The music distracts from the message
- The VO sounds like a robot

Each of those moments is a slop bug. Fix or cut.

## Universal Counter

Best mental model: **show the video to a senior creative director at a top
agency. Would they squint and say "AI made this"? If yes, redesign.** The
goal is not "good for AI" but "indistinguishable from human-crafted."
